White Water Rafting

Our goal is to provide you with the best experience possible. We have been providing unforgettable experiences for over 30 years. Making memories that will last a lifetime is what we do best!

History

Rapids! started floating the river in 1995. We began as a small family operation with one raft and a passion for the river. Over 30 years, we have grown into a premier rafting outfitter while staying true to our roots.

What began with weekend trips for friends has become a legacy. Our team of experienced, certified guides knows every bend of the river. We have guided thousands of families and thrill-seekers through unforgettable rapids.
